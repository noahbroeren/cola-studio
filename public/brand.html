The mark.

Three letters, two circles, one line weight. The Cola Studio monogram is constructed like a garment — measured, cut and woven together. Here is how it works, and how to use it.

Logo variantsAnatomy

Anatomy

C · O · A

  1. C
    The studio

    An outer circle, open on the right — a studio that stays open to the world.

  2. O
    The loop

    The inner circle: sketch, pattern, sample, production — and back again.

  3. A
    The atelier

    Legs at 14.4° from vertical, apex on the outer circle, woven over and under the O like threads.

  4. —
    One line weight

    Every stroke is 3.7% of the mark's diameter. Nothing is thicker, nothing thinner.

Logo system

Variants & lockups

Every variant is generated from the same vector geometry. Download them as SVG (or PNG for the lockups) for packaging, socials and print.

Clear space

Keep a margin of at least ¼ of the mark's diameter on every side. Nothing enters this zone.

Minimum size

Monogram from 16 px (digital) or 8 mm (print). Horizontal lockup from 96 px wide.

Don'ts

Never stretch, rotate, outline, add effects or recolour outside the palette.

Colour

Cola, foam & syrup.

A warm near-black and a soft off-white, with a single caramel accent — used sparingly, like a contrast stitch.

Typography

Poppins, at every weight.

One geometric family, used at its extremes. Headlines mix ExtraLight and Bold within a single line; labels are small, wide and uppercase.
